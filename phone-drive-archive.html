Phone Drive Archive

Internal channel-management tool of an individual YouTube creator · Developer: 윤정식 (individual) · Contact: nataws88@gmail.com

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Internal use only. Phone Drive Archive is not a public product. It is not distributed, sold, or offered to anyone. It runs only on the developer's own Android phone, and only the developer's own Google accounts authorize it (OAuth 2.0).

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the developer produced to the developer's own YouTube channels (RankTime | 순위의 시간 and 1분 신기한 세상).
  • Manages those videos: title, description, tags, language, thumbnail, captions, playlists, scheduled publishing time, and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.
  • Reads the developer's own channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API, YouTube Reporting API) to build a private daily report for the developer.
  • Archives the developer's own large work files (source footage, renders) to the developer's own Google Drive.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using this tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and to the Privacy Policy. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
